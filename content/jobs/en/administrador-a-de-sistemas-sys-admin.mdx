**Modality:** 100% Remote (Spain)  
**Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (billing per project or per hour)  
**Level:** Semi-Senior / Senior

### About the Position

We are looking for a **Systems Administrator** to manage, maintain, and improve the technological infrastructure, ensuring its availability, security, and performance, in collaboration with the IT, development, and support teams.

### Responsibilities

* **Infrastructure Management:** Install, configure, and maintain Linux and Windows servers, both physical and virtualized.
* **Monitoring:** Supervise performance, availability, and incidents, applying the necessary corrective actions.
* **Business Continuity:** Manage backups, disaster recovery, and continuity plans.
* **Access Administration:** Manage users, permissions, and access via Active Directory, LDAP, or IAM.
* **Networking and Security:** Apply security patches and manage networks, firewalls, VPN, DNS, DHCP, and email.
* **Automation:** Automate tasks and deployments through scripting and infrastructure-as-code tools.
* **Support and Documentation:** Document procedures and provide Level 2 and 3 technical support.

### Requirements

* **Experience:** Minimum 4 years of experience in systems administration.
* **Education:** Degree in Computer Science, Telecommunications, or similar, or equivalent experience.
* **Operating Systems:** Solid knowledge of Linux (Debian, Ubuntu, RHEL) and/or Windows Server.
* **Virtualization:** Experience with VMware, Hyper-V, or Proxmox.
* **Networking:** Knowledge of TCP/IP, VLAN, VPN, and firewalls.
* **Scripting:** Proficiency in Bash, PowerShell, or Python.
* **Monitoring Tools:** Experience with Zabbix, Nagios, Grafana, or Prometheus.
* **Soft Skills:** Problem-solving ability, autonomy, and effective team communication.

### Desirable / Valued

* **Cloud Computing:** Experience in AWS, Azure, or GCP.
* **DevOps:** Knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, Ansible, or Terraform.
* **Certifications:** RHCSA, MCSA, AWS, CCNA, or ITIL.
* **Cybersecurity:** Knowledge of ISO 27001, ENS, and GDPR frameworks.
* **Languages:** Technical English.
