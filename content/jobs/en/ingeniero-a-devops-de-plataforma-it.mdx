**Modality:** 100% Remote (Spain)  
**Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (project-based or hourly billing)  
**Level:** Semi-Senior

### About the position

We are looking for a **DevOps Engineer** to manage systems and services that support mobile and IoT platforms, automating operations and collaborating on infrastructure availability and performance.

### Responsibilities

*   **Platform Administration:** Manage and improve the availability of IT platforms supporting mobile and IoT services.
*   **Infrastructure Operations:** Operate Linux systems, VMware virtualized environments, and cloud-deployed services.
*   **Data Management:** Manage data and messaging components, including Oracle, MongoDB, Redis, and RabbitMQ.
*   **Automation:** Automate operational tasks, configurations, and deployments using scripting and administration tools.
*   **Technical Support:** Provide support for integrations via network protocols and REST/SOAP APIs.
*   **Monitoring:** Monitor services, investigate incidents, and document corrective actions.

### Requirements

**Experience and Education**
*   Minimum 4 years of experience in mobile networks, IoT, and IT platform environments.
*   Degree in Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or equivalent.
*   B2 level or equivalent in spoken and written English.

**Technical Knowledge**
*   **Architecture and Networks:** Knowledge of IoT service architecture, 2G to 5G mobile networks, and OCS, SMSC, HLR, HSS, and SMSHR services.
*   **Systems and Virtualization:** Advanced Linux administration (especially Red Hat) and knowledge of VMware vSphere virtualization.
*   **Cloud and Networks:** Experience with cloud services in AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud and IP networks.
*   **Databases and Messaging:** Advanced knowledge of Oracle, MongoDB, and Redis, and experience with RabbitMQ.
*   **Protocols:** Knowledge of MAP, DIAMETER, CAMEL, and ISUP; HTTP/HTTPS and REST/SOAP APIs.
*   **Monitoring:** Experience with tools such as Checkmk, Icinga, Splunk, or ATIVA.
*   **Automation:** Scripting with Bash, Python, or Groovy and administration and automation tools such as Puppet, Ansible, or Jenkins.

**Soft Skills**
*   Clear communication, analytical thinking, and capacity for synthesis.

### Desirable / Valued

*   Experience working with hybrid infrastructure, virtualized environments, and cloud services.
*   Ability to transversally analyze incidents in IoT platforms and services.
