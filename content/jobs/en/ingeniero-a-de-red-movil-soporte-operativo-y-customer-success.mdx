**Modality:** 100% Remote (Spain)  
**Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (project-based or hourly billing)  
**Level:** Intermediate

### About the Position

We are looking for an operational support professional in mobile and IoT networks to manage incidents and technical queries, with the ability to analyze connectivity platforms and support customers in their daily use.

### Responsibilities

*   Handle and track incidents related to mobile data, voice, and SMS services.
*   Support the diagnosis of roaming, IP connectivity, and mobile device operation.
*   Consult IR21 documentation and evidence from monitoring and troubleshooting tools.
*   Collaborate with teams responsible for KITE, Cisco Jasper, and other IoT platforms.
*   Record and communicate the status of incidents and the actions taken.

### Requirements

*   **Experience and Education:**
    *   Minimum of 4 years of experience in mobile networks and IoT.
    *   Degree in Telecommunications Engineering or equivalent qualification.
*   **Technical Knowledge:**
    *   Large-scale IoT platforms and solutions such as KITE, Cisco Jasper, and 1NCE.
    *   2G, 3G, 4G, 5G, NB-IoT, LTE-M technologies, IP networks, and mobile devices.
    *   MAP, DIAMETER, ISUP, SIP, and CAMEL protocols; voice core and roaming.
    *   Familiarity with UNIX/Ubuntu, IR21 documentation, and VPN, MPLS, and IPsec networks.
    *   Experience in diagnosing data, voice, and SMS incidents.
*   **Languages and Competencies:**
    *   Spoken and written English at B2 level or equivalent.
    *   Effective communication, analytical and synthesis skills, and customer orientation.

### Desirable

*   Use of tools such as Empirix, ATIVA, Devo, Splunk, Wireshark, or GlobalRoamer.
*   Knowledge of SM-DP and SM-SR and Oracle or MongoDB databases.
