### Vacancy Details

*   **Modality:** 100% Remote (Spain)
*   **Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (billing per project or per hour)
*   **Level:** Semi-Senior

### About the Position

We are looking for a **Private Mobile Network Engineer** to design, integrate, and maintain 4G and 5G solutions for IoT environments, with a specialized focus on mobile architecture, network virtualization, and incident resolution.

### Responsibilities

*   Participate in the design, integration, and support of private 4G/5G mobile networks.
*   Analyze core elements, signaling, and mobile services related to IoT connectivity.
*   Review network virtualization architectures and Kubernetes-based environments.
*   Diagnose incidents and validate their resolution using troubleshooting and roaming tools.
*   Integrate monitoring tasks with tools such as Nokia Neact, ServiceNow, or Nagios.
*   Coordinate technical work, documentation, and tracking of project objectives.

### Requirements

**Education and Experience**
*   Degree in Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent.
*   Minimum 4 years of experience in private 4G and 5G network engineering.
*   C1 level of English or equivalent; fluent oral and written communication in Spanish and English.

**Technical Knowledge**
*   **Networks:** Advanced knowledge of IoT networks, 2G to 5G mobile networks, NB-IoT, LTE-M, and networking.
*   **Core and Databases:** Experience with STP, SSP, SCP and mobile core/databases such as Nokia UDR, Oracle VSTP, Huawei UDB, and Ericsson HLR/HSS.
*   **Signaling:** Knowledge of SS7, ISUP, INAP, TCAP, SCCP, CS1, CAP, SIGTRAN, CAMEL, and DIAMETER.
*   **Services and Virtualization:** Proficiency in MSC/VLR, CMC, SGSN/MME/GGSN/PDNGW, network virtualization, and Kubernetes.
*   **Standards and Tools:** Handling of CCITT, GSMA, and 3GPP documentation; use of Empirix, ATIVA, Devo, and GlobalRoamer tools.
*   **Integration:** Experience with Nokia Neact, ServiceNow, or Nagios.

**Soft Skills**
*   Analytical and project management skills.
*   Autonomy and strong results orientation.

### Desirable

*   Experience coordinating incidents involving private networks and core elements from different vendors.
*   Practical experience in projects with network function virtualization and Kubernetes.
