# Data Engineer

## About the Position
We are looking for a **Data Engineer** to design and maintain robust and scalable data pipelines that power the company's analytical and AI products.

## Responsibilities
* Design, build, and maintain ETL/ELT pipelines.
* Model and optimize data stores (Data Warehouse / Data Lake).
* Ensure data quality, governance, and traceability.
* Collaborate with analytics, product, and GenAI teams to expose data efficiently.
* Automate data ingestion and orchestration processes.

## Requirements
* Minimum 3 years of experience in data engineering.
* Proficiency in **SQL** and **Python** (or Scala).
* Experience with orchestration tools (**Airflow, Dagster, Prefect**).
* Knowledge of cloud platforms (**AWS, Azure, or GCP**).
* Experience with Big Data technologies (**Spark, Kafka, Databricks**).

## Desirable Skills
* Experience with **dbt** (data build tool).
* Knowledge of **Data Mesh** or **Lakehouse** architectures.
* Cloud certifications (AWS/Azure/GCP Data Engineer).
