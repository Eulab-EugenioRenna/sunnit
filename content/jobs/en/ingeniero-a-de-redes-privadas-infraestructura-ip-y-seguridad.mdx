**Level:** Intermediate

### About the Position

We are looking for an engineer to deploy and maintain IP connectivity for 4G/5G private networks at customer sites, combining routing, switching, security, balancing, and container orchestration platforms.

### Responsibilities

*   **Design and Deployment:** LAN/WAN interconnections for 4G/5G private networks at customer installations.
*   **Network Configuration:** Management of BGP/OSPF routing, VRF, switching, aggregation, and monitoring on Cisco, Juniper, and Nokia equipment.
*   **Traffic and Tunnel Management:** Application of Quality of Service (QoS) policies and management of IPSec, SSL, and GRE tunnels.
*   **Security and Balancing:** Implementation of Fortinet security functions, F5 balancing, hardening measures, and microsegmentation.
*   **Modern Architectures:** Participation in network architecture deployments for Kubernetes and Red Hat OpenShift.
*   **Monitoring and Documentation:** Integration with Nokia Neact, ServiceNow, or Nagios, and detailed documentation of incidents and actions.

### Requirements

**Experience and Education**
*   Minimum 4 years of experience in 4G/5G private networks and network architecture deployments for Kubernetes.
*   Degree in Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent.
*   C1 level English or equivalent; ability to communicate fluently in Spanish and English.

**Technical Knowledge**
*   **Architectures:** Nokia NDAC, Nokia MPW, and Ericsson EP5G.
*   **Networking:** Advanced knowledge of IPv6, BGP, OSPF, VRF, and switching in Cisco, Juniper, and Nokia.
*   **Connectivity and Balancing:** Experience with Cisco QoS, IPSec/SSL/GRE tunnels, and F5 balancing (including iRules).
*   **Fortinet Security:** Management of layer 3-7 firewalls, IPS, VPN, antivirus, FortiAuthenticator, and FortiAnalyzer.
*   **Infrastructure:** Experience in LAN/WAN communications, hardening, perimeter security, and microsegmentation.
*   **Containers:** Experience in network orchestration with Kubernetes, preferably OpenShift.
*   **Management Tools:** Integration with Nokia Neact, ServiceNow, or Nagios.

**Competencies**
*   Analytical capacity, project management, and goal-oriented mindset.

### Desirable

*   Knowledge of Athonet and Casa Systems architectures.
*   Experience in customer environments combining private mobile networks, advanced security, and balancing.
