### About the Position

We are looking for an IoT operations professional to execute provisioning tasks and mobile connectivity support, focusing on service monitoring, incident resolution, and working with **KITE** and **Cisco Jasper** platforms.

### Responsibilities

*   Perform provisioning operations and monitoring of IoT services.
*   Verify basic parameters and behaviors of 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G networks and their radio access network (RAN).
*   Support the identification of incidents associated with international roaming and steering via network or SIM.
*   Review connectivity through public and private networks and VPN, MPLS, and IPsec solutions.
*   Record the actions performed and communicate the results clearly.

### Requirements

*   **Experience:** Minimum 4 years in mobile networks and IoT.
*   **Technical Knowledge:**
    *   2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G technologies, RAN, and international roaming.
    *   Network and SIM roaming steering mechanisms.
    *   Service provisioning with KITE and Cisco Jasper.
    *   Public and private networks, VPN, MPLS, IPsec, and Internet.
*   **Languages:** Spoken and written English at B2 level or equivalent.
*   **Skills:** Good communication, analytical capacity, synthesis, and critical thinking.

### Desirable

*   Greater autonomy in international roaming operations and multi-service provisioning.
*   Experience collaborating with network and platform support teams.
