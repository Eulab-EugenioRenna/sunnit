# GenAI Engineer

## About the Role
We are looking for a **GenAI Engineer** to design, build, and deploy solutions based on large language models (LLMs) and generative AI architectures, integrating them into real products and business workflows.

## Responsibilities
* **Design and implement** RAG (*Retrieval-Augmented Generation*) pipelines.
* **Develop and integrate** LLM-based agents (function calling, tool use, orchestration).
* **Evaluate and tune** models (fine-tuning, prompt engineering, few-shot learning).
* **Optimize** costs and inference latency in production.
* **Collaborate** with product and data science teams to define use cases.

## Requirements
* **Experience:** Minimum of 2-3 years working with LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, open source models).
* **Languages and Frameworks:** Solid knowledge of Python and frameworks such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, or similar.
* **Storage:** Experience with vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, pgvector).
* **Architecture:** Knowledge of REST APIs and microservices-based architectures.
* **Languages:** Professional English (B2+ level).

## Desirable
* Experience with cloud deployment (AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, GCP Vertex AI).
* Knowledge of MLOps / LLMOps.
* Publications or open source projects related to generative AI.
