**Modality:** 100% Remote (Spain)  
**Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (billing per project or per hour)  
**Level:** Intermediate

### About the Position

We are looking for a mobile network operations specialist to analyze incidents and collaborate on the maintenance of Telco and IoT services, focusing on core platforms and signaling protocols.

### Responsibilities

*   Execute diagnostic and tracking tasks for voice, SMS, and data incidents in the mobile network.
*   Analyze signaling traces and service behavior over 2G to 5G networks.
*   Collaborate in the operation of STP, SSP, SCP, and UDR/HLR/HSS platforms.
*   Support roaming tests with GlobalRoamer and troubleshooting activities using technical tools.
*   Prepare technical documentation, incident reports, and test evidence.

### Requirements

**Education and Experience**
*   Minimum 4 years of experience in mobile networks and IoT.
*   Degree in Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or equivalent.
*   Spoken and written English at C1 level or equivalent.

**Technical Knowledge**
*   **Networks and Platforms:** Knowledge of 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G, NB-IoT, LTE-M networks and STP, SSP, and SCP platforms.
*   **Environments:** Experience with Nokia UDR, Oracle VSTP, Huawei UDB, Ericsson HLR/HSS, and SS7 Datatronics environments.
*   **Protocols:** Knowledge of SS7, ISUP, INAP, TCAP, SCCP, CS1, CAP, SIGTRAN, CAMEL, DIAMETER, and SIP.
*   **Infrastructure:** Knowledge of MSC/VLR, CMC, SGSN/MME/GGSN/PDNGW.
*   **Tools:** Ability to use GSMA/3GPP documentation and tools such as Empirix, ATIVA, Devo, or GlobalRoamer.

**Skills**
*   Analytical skills, communication, and problem-solving ability.

### Desirable

*   Specialized knowledge of STP/DRA, UDR, and Ericsson or Mavenir IMS.
*   Experience with virtualized or containerized network functions (VNF/CNF).
