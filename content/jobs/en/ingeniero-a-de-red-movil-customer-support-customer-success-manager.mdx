**Modality:** 100% Remote (Spain)  
**Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (project-based or hourly billing)  
**Level:** Semi-Senior

### About the Position

We are looking for a **mobile network engineer** with a focus on technical support and customer success to operate IoT services, analyze incidents, and ensure effective communication between platforms, operators, and professional users.

### Responsibilities

*   **Technical Support:** Provide support to customers and internal teams regarding mobile IoT platforms and services.
*   **Incident Resolution:** Analyze and resolve data, voice, and SMS issues, ensuring traceability and clear communication during follow-up.
*   **Connectivity Diagnosis:** Identify roaming and connectivity problems using protocols, IR21 documentation, and troubleshooting tools.
*   **Platform Operation:** Collaborate in the management of high-volume devices through platforms such as KITE, Cisco Jasper, and 1NCE.
*   **Network Analysis:** Review profile switching scenarios and connectivity across public, private, and VPN networks.
*   **Customer Experience:** Contribute to service stability through technical documentation and continuous monitoring.

### Requirements

**Experience and Education**
*   Degree in Telecommunications Engineering or equivalent qualification.
*   Minimum 4 years of experience in mobile networks and IoT.
*   C1 level or equivalent in spoken and written English.

**Technical Knowledge**
*   **Mobile Technologies:** 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G, NB-IoT, and LTE-M networks, IP networks, and mobile devices.
*   **IoT Platforms:** Experience in high-volume management with KITE, Cisco Jasper, or 1NCE.
*   **Protocols and Core:** Knowledge of MAP, DIAMETER, ISUP, SIP, and CAMEL, as well as voice core and roaming.
*   **Troubleshooting:** Handling of IR21 documentation and diagnosis of data, voice, and SMS.
*   **Systems and Networks:** UNIX/Ubuntu, VPN networks, MPLS, IPsec, and Internet.
*   **Tools:** Empirix, ATIVA, Devo, Splunk, Wireshark, or GlobalRoamer.
*   **Profile Management:** Knowledge of operator profile exchange systems (SM-DP and SM-SR).
*   **Databases:** Basic knowledge of Oracle and MongoDB.

**Soft Skills**
*   Excellent oral and written communication skills.
*   Analytical skills and critical thinking.

### Desirable

*   Experience in transversal troubleshooting between IoT platforms, mobile services, and IP networks.
*   Ability to coordinate support requests and perform technical follow-up directly with customers.
