**Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (project-based or hourly billing)  
**Level:** Senior

### About the Position

We are looking for a **senior DevOps engineer** to operate and evolve the IT platform that supports telecommunications and IoT services, combining Linux systems, cloud, automation, data, and messaging.

### Responsibilities

*   **Technical Leadership:** Lead the operation and technical evolution of IT platforms associated with mobile and IoT services.
*   **Infrastructure Administration:** Administer Red Hat Linux environments, VMware vSphere, and cloud infrastructure services.
*   **Data and Messaging Management:** Ensure the operation of Oracle, MongoDB, Redis, and RabbitMQ and their integration with services.
*   **Automation:** Drive automation through the use of Bash, Python, Groovy, Puppet, Ansible, and Jenkins.
*   **Incident Resolution:** Coordinate the resolution of complex platform, network, monitoring, and API incidents.
*   **Standards:** Establish technical documentation and operational best practices for the team.

### Requirements

**Experience and Education**
*   Minimum **8 years of experience** in mobile and IoT networks with technical responsibility over IT platforms.
*   Degree in Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or equivalent.
*   Spoken and written English at a **B2** level or equivalent.

**Technical Knowledge**
*   **Telecommunications:** IoT service architecture, 2G to 5G mobile networks, and OCS, SMSC, HLR, HSS, and SMSHR services.
*   **Systems and Cloud:** Advanced administration of Linux (especially Red Hat), VMware vSphere virtualization, cloud services (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud), and IP networks.
*   **Databases and Messaging:** Advanced knowledge of Oracle, MongoDB, and Redis, as well as experience with RabbitMQ.
*   **Protocols and APIs:** Knowledge of MAP, DIAMETER, CAMEL, and ISUP; HTTP/HTTPS and REST/SOAP APIs.
*   **Monitoring:** Experience with tools such as Checkmk, Icinga, Splunk, or ATIVA.
*   **Automation:** Scripting with Bash, Python, or Groovy and administration tools such as Puppet, Ansible, or Jenkins.

**Soft Skills**
*   Clear communication, analytical thinking, and capacity for synthesis.

### Desirable

*   Experience coordinating environments that combine mobile services, databases, messaging, and cloud.
*   Ability to lead diagnostics and technical actions in high-complexity platforms.
