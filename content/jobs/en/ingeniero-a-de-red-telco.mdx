**Modality:** 100% Remote (Spain)  
**Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (project-based or hourly billing)  
**Level:** Semi-Senior

### About the Position

We are looking for a **Telco network engineer** to maintain and improve mobile and IoT platforms, analyzing complex core, signaling, and voice, SMS, and data service incidents.

### Responsibilities

* **Operation and Analysis:** Analyze, operate, and resolve incidents in mobile and IoT networks and telecommunications core platforms.
* **Network Management:** Work with intelligent networks STP/SSP/SCP, UDR/HLR/HSS elements, and mobile voice, SMS, and data services.
* **Diagnosis:** Diagnose signaling problems using SS7, DIAMETER, SIP protocols and related technologies.
* **Testing and Validation:** Participate in interoperability and roaming tests and trace analysis using specialized tools.
* **Documentation:** Document tests, incidents, and changes according to GSMA, 3GPP specifications and applicable standards.

### Requirements

**Education and Experience**
* Degree in Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or equivalent.
* Minimum of 4 years of experience in mobile and IoT networks.
* Spoken and written English at C1 level or equivalent.

**Technical Knowledge**
* **Networks and Networking:** Advanced knowledge of IoT, networking, and 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G, NB-IoT, and LTE-M mobile networks.
* **Platforms:** 
    * Intelligent Network: STP, SSP, and SCP.
    * Mobile databases: Nokia UDR, Oracle VSTP, Huawei UDB, and Ericsson HLR/HSS.
    * Datatronics SS7 stack.
    * VNF/CNF environments.
    * Ericsson or Mavenir IMS.
* **Protocols:** SS7, ISUP, INAP, TCAP, SCCP, CS1, CAP, SIGTRAN, CAMEL, DIAMETER, and SIP.
* **Mobile Services:** Voice (MSC/VLR), SMS (CMC), and data (SGSN/MME/GGSN/PDNGW).
* **Tools and Standards:** 
    * CCITT, GSMA, and 3GPP technical documentation.
    * Troubleshooting with Empirix, ATIVA, and Devo.
    * Roaming tests with GlobalRoamer.
    * STP/DRA (Oracle or Ericsson) and UDR (Nokia, Ericsson, or Huawei).

**Soft Skills**
* Effective technical communication.
* Capacity for analysis, synthesis, and problem-solving.

### Desirable

* Participation in complex Telco projects or multi-vendor environments.
* Combined experience in incident resolution, roaming tests, and network documentation.
