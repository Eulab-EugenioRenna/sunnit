**Modality:** 100% Remote (Spain)  
**Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (billing per project or per hour)  
**Level:** Intermediate

### About the Position

We are looking for an **L1/L2 IT Support Technician** to handle user requests and incidents, resolve workstation and connectivity problems, and coordinate escalations with Level 3 infrastructure teams.

### Responsibilities

*   **Incident Management:** Register and classify tickets received via phone, email, chat, and support tools.
*   **Level 1 Support (L1):** Resolve frequent incidents regarding accounts, passwords, email, printers, VPN, and applications.
*   **Level 2 Support (L2):** Diagnose hardware, software, and network problems, including equipment installation and imaging.
*   **Systems Administration:** Manage users and permissions in Active Directory, Microsoft 365, and Entra ID.
*   **Technical Support:** Provide remote and on-site support, manage MDM devices, and coordinate escalations to L3.
*   **Quality and Documentation:** Meet SLAs, document solutions, and contribute to the knowledge base.
*   **Operational Management:** Manage assets, licenses, and onboarding/offboarding processes.

### Requirements

*   **Experience:** Minimum 4 years in technical support and L1/L2 help desk.
*   **Education:** Studies in Computer Science, Telecommunications, or similar, or equivalent experience.
*   **Operating Systems:** Proficiency in Windows 10/11, macOS, and Microsoft 365.
*   **Identity Management:** Experience with Active Directory and user management.
*   **Networking:** Knowledge of TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, Wi-Fi, and VPN.
*   **Ticketing Tools:** Experience with ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Zendesk, or GLPI.
*   **Languages:** English level B1/B2, depending on project needs.
*   **Soft Skills:** Excellent communication, user orientation, prioritization ability, and teamwork.

### Desirable

*   **Certifications:** CompTIA A+, ITIL Foundation, Microsoft 365 Fundamentals, or MD-102.
*   **Advanced Tools:** Experience with Intune, SCCM, and remote support tools.
*   **Additional Techniques:** Knowledge of PowerShell, Bash, Linux, and cybersecurity fundamentals.
*   **Environments:** Experience in environments with SLAs and multi-client support.
