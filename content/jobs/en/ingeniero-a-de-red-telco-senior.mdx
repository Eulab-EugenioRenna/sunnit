**Level:** Senior

### About the position

We are looking for a **Senior Telco Network Engineer** to lead engineering, operation, and diagnostic tasks for mobile networks and core platforms, with experience in signaling, voice, SMS, data, and IoT.

### Responsibilities

*   **Incident Resolution:** Lead investigations and resolution of critical incidents on mobile network and core platforms.
*   **Solution Design:** Define and review solutions for STP, DRA, UDR, IMS, and VNF/CNF architectures.
*   **Technical Analysis:** Analyze signaling and voice, SMS, data, and roaming services using specialized protocols and tools.
*   **Coordination:** Coordinate tests, trace analysis, and actions with telecommunications teams and providers.
*   **Documentation:** Prepare documentation and technical recommendations following GSMA and 3GPP standards.

### Requirements

**Experience and Education**
*   Minimum 8 years of experience in mobile networks and IoT, with participation in complex Telco projects.
*   Degree in Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or equivalent.
*   Spoken and written English at C1 level or equivalent.

**Technical Knowledge**
*   **Mobile Networks:** Advanced knowledge of networking and 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G, NB-IoT, and LTE-M networks.
*   **Platforms:**
    *   Intelligent Network STP, SSP, and SCP.
    *   Mobile databases: Nokia UDR, Oracle VSTP, Huawei UDB, Ericsson HLR/HSS, and Datatronics SS7 stack.
    *   STP/DRA (Oracle or Ericsson), UDR (Nokia, Ericsson, or Huawei), and VNF/CNF environments.
    *   Ericsson or Mavenir IMS.
*   **Protocols:** SS7, ISUP, INAP, TCAP, SCCP, CS1, CAP, SIGTRAN, CAMEL, DIAMETER, and SIP.
*   **Mobile Services:** Voice (MSC/VLR), SMS (CMC), and data (SGSN/MME/GGSN/PDNGW).
*   **Tools and Standards:**
    *   CCITT, GSMA, and 3GPP technical documentation, test plans, and technical reports.
    *   Troubleshooting with Empirix, ATIVA, and Devo.
    *   Roaming tests with GlobalRoamer.

**Soft Skills**
*   Effective technical communication.
*   Capacity for analysis, synthesis, and problem-solving.

### Desirable

*   Technical leadership in high-complexity Telco initiatives.
*   Specialized experience in interactions between IMS, signaling, UDR, and virtualized platforms.
