## Position Details

*   **Modality:** 100% Remote (Spain)
*   **Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Self-employed (project-based or hourly billing)
*   **Level:** Semi-Senior

### About the Role

We are looking for an **IoT Provisioning Specialist** to manage the provisioning and support of mobile connectivity and IoT services, working with specialized platforms and public and private network environments.

### Responsibilities

*   Execute and supervise provisioning processes for IoT services and devices on **KITE** and **Cisco Jasper** platforms.
*   Analyze connectivity configurations and behaviors in **2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G** mobile networks.
*   Review international roaming scenarios and roaming steering mechanisms based on network and SIM.
*   Collaborate in resolving incidents related to connectivity, services, and access to public and private networks.
*   Document configurations, incidents, and technical actions for the involved teams.

### Requirements

*   **Experience:** Minimum 4 years in mobile networks and IoT.
*   **Mobile Networks:** Knowledge of 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G architectures, as well as RAN in these technologies.
*   **Roaming:** Knowledge of international roaming and roaming steering via network and SIM mechanisms.
*   **Platforms:** Experience in service provisioning on KITE and Cisco Jasper platforms.
*   **Connectivity:** Knowledge of public and private networks, VPN, MPLS, IPsec, and Internet.
*   **Languages:** C1 level or equivalent spoken and written English.
*   **Skills:** Effective technical communication, capacity for synthesis, analysis, and critical thinking.

### Desirable

*   Experience in provisioning and roaming scenarios involving different operators.
*   Ability to coordinate technical incidents between connectivity and platform teams.
