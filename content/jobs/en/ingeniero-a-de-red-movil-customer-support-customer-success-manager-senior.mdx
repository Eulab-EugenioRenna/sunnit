**Level:** Senior

### About the position

We are looking for a senior specialist in mobile networks and IoT to combine the resolution of complex incidents with technical guidance for clients operating platforms and large fleets of connected devices.

### Responsibilities

- **Lead the diagnosis** of complex data, voice, SMS, and roaming incidents in high-scale IoT platforms.
- **Coordinate** technical stakeholders and clients until the resolution and stabilization of services.
- **Analyze** the behavior of protocols, voice core, mobile architecture, and provisioning platforms.
- **Investigate problems** using tools such as Empirix, ATIVA, Devo, Splunk, Wireshark, and GlobalRoamer.
- **Provide support** for SM-DP/SM-SR profile exchange scenarios and VPN, MPLS, and IPsec interconnections.
- **Document** findings, actions, and service improvement recommendations.

### Requirements

**Experience and Education**
- Minimum 8 years of experience in mobile networks and IoT.
- Degree in Telecommunications Engineering or equivalent qualification.
- Spoken and written English at C1 level or equivalent.

**Technical Knowledge**
- **IoT Platforms:** Experience with high-volume platforms and knowledge of KITE, Cisco Jasper, or 1NCE.
- **Mobile Networks:** Knowledge of 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G, NB-IoT, and LTE-M networks, IP networks, and mobile devices.
- **Protocols and Core:** Mastery of MAP, DIAMETER, ISUP, SIP, and CAMEL protocols, as well as voice core and roaming.
- **Troubleshooting:** Handling of IR21 documentation and experience in resolving data, voice, and SMS problems.
- **Infrastructure and Tools:** 
    - Knowledge of UNIX/Ubuntu, VPN networks, MPLS, IPsec, and Internet.
    - Proficiency in Empirix, ATIVA, Devo, Splunk, Wireshark, or GlobalRoamer.
    - Basic knowledge of Oracle and MongoDB databases.
- **Operator Profiles:** Advanced knowledge of profile exchange via SM-DP and SM-SR.

**Soft Skills**
- Professional communication, technical leadership, synthesis capacity, and critical thinking.

### Valued Experience

- Experience coordinating support and technical relations with clients regarding large-scale IoT deployments.
- Specialization in cross-protocol analysis, roaming, and operator profiles.
