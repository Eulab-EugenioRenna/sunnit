# Cloud Architect

## About the Position
We are looking for a **Cloud Architect** to lead the design of scalable, secure, and cost-efficient cloud architectures, guiding technical teams in infrastructure best practices.

## Responsibilities
* **Architecture Design:** Create cloud architectures (multi-cloud or hybrid) aligned with business needs.
* **Technical Standards:** Define security, scalability, and resilience standards.
* **Automation:** Lead the adoption of Infrastructure as Code (IaC).
* **Optimization:** Manage and optimize cloud costs (FinOps).
* **Mentorship:** Advise development and DevOps teams on architectural decisions.

## Requirements
* **Experience:** Minimum of 5 years in cloud architecture.
* **Platforms:** Mastery of at least one cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP); multiple are valued.
* **IaC Tools:** Experience with Terraform, CloudFormation, or similar tools.
* **Technical Knowledge:** Solid knowledge of networking, security, and containers (Kubernetes, Docker).
* **Certifications:** Architect-level cloud certifications (e.g., AWS Solutions Architect Professional).

## Preferred Qualifications
* Experience in large-scale cloud migrations.
* Knowledge of serverless and event-driven architectures.
* Experience leading technical teams.
