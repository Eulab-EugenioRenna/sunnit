# Network Engineer

**Mode:** 100% Remote (Spain)  
**Collaboration Type:** Freelance / Independent Contractor (project-based or hourly billing)  
**Level:** Semi-Senior / Senior

### About the Position

We are looking for a Network Engineer to design, implement, and maintain LAN, WAN, and WLAN infrastructures, ensuring the connectivity, availability, performance, and security of services.

### Responsibilities

*   **Design and Maintenance:** Design, configure, and maintain LAN, WAN, and WLAN networks.
*   **Hardware Administration:** Manage routers, switches, firewalls, and load balancers.
*   **Connectivity:** Manage VPN, SD-WAN, and connectivity between sites.
*   **Technical Support:** Monitor the network and resolve Level 2/3 technical incidents.
*   **Security and Segmentation:** Implement ACL policies, segmentation, VLAN, and NAC.
*   **Optimization:** Plan migrations, expansions, and updates; automate repetitive configurations.
*   **Document Management:** Maintain diagrams, inventories, and documentation, collaborating closely with providers and operators.

### Requirements

*   **Experience:** Minimum 4 years of experience in network administration or engineering.
*   **Education:** Degree in Computer Science, Telecommunications, or similar, or equivalent experience.
*   **Technical Knowledge:**
    *   Solid knowledge of TCP/IP, routing, and switching (including OSPF, BGP, STP, and VLAN).
    *   Experience with Cisco, Juniper, Fortinet, Palo Alto, Aruba equipment or equivalents.
    *   Proficiency in firewalls, IPSec/SSL VPN, DNS, DHCP, and NAT.
*   **Tools:** Proficiency with PRTG, Zabbix, SolarWinds, or Wireshark.
*   **Languages:** Ability to work with technical documentation in English.
*   **Soft Skills:** Analytical thinking, clear communication, autonomy, and problem-solving under pressure.

### Desirable

*   **Certifications:** CCNA, CCNP, JNCIA, or NSE.
*   **Cloud Networking:** Experience with AWS VPC, Azure VNet, or hybrid networks.
*   **Specializations:** Knowledge of SD-WAN, wireless networks, and Voice over IP.
*   **Automation:** Python, Ansible, or Netmiko.
*   **Regulations:** Knowledge of ISO 27001, ENS, and cybersecurity best practices.
